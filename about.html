QC Calibration Processes

Phase 01 // Loom Thread Checks

We monitor the yarn warp indices and spool friction counts to verify that needles do not snag yarn fibers under fast loom sweeps.

Phase 02 // Heel Abrasion Scrapes

We log raw friction scrapes using high-density steel plates to confirm that heel panels resist hole splits during walking sweeps.

Phase 03 // Wet Wash Shrinkage

Socks are washed repeatedly at high temperatures to evaluate structural shrinkage ratios and ensure long-term shape retention.

Phase 04 // Gradient Pressure Mapping

Gradient compression values are mapped across different leg sizes to confirm steady blood flow retention under running loads.
