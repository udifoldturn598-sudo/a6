Yarn Slides: Heel Friction Curves

I. Introduction to Footwear Comfort & Knits

Custom socks heel friction and thread slide coefficients are vital for skin abrasion safety. ComfortStep Works runs friction tests on all heel patches. By testing thread coatings, we secure heel wear rates. To evaluate this in depth, we establish a baseline testing model under standard environmental conditions. Every loop density check and active spandex compression calibration is verified multiple times across different design batches to maintain accuracy. Our technical curators catalog the yarn patterns and structural mesh clearances. We verify both steel and fiber panels to identify warping sags, logging moisture levels across all loops. This commitment to footwear integrity ensures a reliable stride speed track, anti-sag posturing, and long-term socks durability.
